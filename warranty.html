Warranty periods

The cover recorded for your job applies.

Your receipt or warranty PDF is the clearest record. These are the normal MH Connect options.

Phone screens

Standard: normally 14 days technical warranty.
Premium or foldable: normally 30 days where selected.

Battery & charging

Normally 14 days technical warranty, or 30 days where the longer option was agreed and recorded.

Watch batteries

Renata packages: Basic £10 / 1 year, Plus £15 / 3 years, or Premium £20 / 5 years.

Accessories

Normally a 14-day faulty-item exchange after inspection. This is not change-of-mind cover for used items.

These shop warranty terms do not affect your statutory rights. The exact warranty shown on your receipt or certificate takes priority.

Usually covered

Technical faults with our fitted part or work.

  • Confirmed technical fault with the fitted part during the recorded period
  • Confirmed fitting fault caused by our repair work
  • Watch-battery failure within the selected battery package
  • Manufacturing fault with an accessory after inspection

Usually not covered

New damage or unrelated faults.

  • Cracks, drops, pressure marks, bending, impact or misuse
  • Liquid damage, corrosion or damage after collection
  • Charging cables, plugs, software, board faults or other unrelated issues
  • Damage caused by a customer-supplied part or an incompatible accessory

Important special cases

Some work has different cover.

Liquid-damaged devices

Inspection, cleaning or repair is best-effort only. Hidden corrosion and future liquid-related failures are not covered by a long-term warranty.

Customer-supplied parts

Only our fitting work may be covered. MH Connect does not provide a warranty for the supplied part, its quality or compatibility.

Foldable phones

Technical cover does not include hinge damage, folding pressure, internal pressure marks, impact, cracks, liquid or physical stress.
